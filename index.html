Redefining What’s Possible in Independent Wealth Management
Redefining What’s Possible in Independent Wealth Management
Over the past two decades, the independent financial advisory landscape has evolved significantly — bringing with it a unique opportunity for firms committed to preserving their independence and authenticity.
Today’s environment, shaped by a rising generation of leadership, democratized access to institutional-grade technology, and unprecedented transparency in valuations and deal structures, offers forward-looking firms the chance not just to survive this era of mass aggregation — but to thrive in it.
At AGC Plover Hill, we challenge the conventional thinking that internal succession from G1 to G2 is impractical or economically out of reach. We reject the notion that independent firms must compromise their identity to stay competitive.
We believe in a different path — one where independence is not only preserved but amplified. With the right capital, strategy, and partnership, advisory firms can grow, evolve, and define success on their own terms.

Common Challenges
Facing RIAs Today

Legacy Preservation

  • G1 seeks flexible exit timelines for retirement
  • G1 wants a structured path to G2 earning into partnership
  • G1 desires fair market value for exit liquidity
  • G2 lacks access to capital resources to buy out G1 at market terms
  • G1’s only alternative is to sell to outside parties, likely relinquishing independence
  • G1 sometimes hesitant to make changes that may “rock the boat”
  • Client messaging requires careful management during organizational changes

Growth Acceleration

  • G2’s path to equity ownership is sometimes opaque, with the potential for misaligned incentives
  • G2’s ascent to leadership may sometimes benefit from outside advice and guidance
  • Organic growth and M&A strategies are difficult to self-fund or finance
  • Operational optimization requires continual investments, especially with rapid growth
  • HR and human capital strategies that will promote long term growth
  • Ever changing technology and service offerings are challenging to analyze

Leadership
Rob Martin
Managing Director, AGC Equity Partners
In 2008, Rob began his finance career at Symmetry Partners, climbing the ranks from Compliance Associate to Director of Marketing to President. Tasked with leading a new RIA initiative in 2014, he co-founded Apella, which grew from $80 million to $3.5 billion in AUM by June 2023. Under his leadership, Apella completed 13 transactions and launched 10 offices nationwide, solidifying its status as one of the fastest-growing RIAs. Rob holds degrees in Psychology from the University of Richmond and the University of New Haven.
Outside work, he serves on the board of the CFM Foundation and enjoys summers in Narragansett, RI, and skiing in the winter.
Najib Canaan
US Operating Partner, AGC Equity Partners
Najib has over 30 years of investment experience serving in myriad executive roles such as the CEO of Kingswood US Holdings, the founder and CIO of Marinus Capital Advisor, and as Managing Director and Head of Structured Finance at GSO Capital. He further served as Executive Managing Director and Co-Head of International Markets at Nomura Securities International and Co-Head of Fixed Income (Europe) for Nomura International PLC. During his off-time, Najib enjoys biking, swimming, traveling and spending time with his wife and three kids.
